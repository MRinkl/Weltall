; The house in Lahan, where the man plays rock-paper-scissors and his wife says her lines. Once a visit has
; lost three rounds, she offers, talked to, to have a word with him; once she has, his hand loses to Fei's
; for the rest of the visit.
map 7
option fix.rps_helper

; His talk script: the test after his "One, two..." choice, and the jump after a lost round's "Lost 50G.".
detour @0x024A to Hands If var[014] == #1 else @0x0329
detour @0x0563 to Lost Jump @0x059B
; Her talk script, once she has turned toward Fei: the test between her lines after the Badge and before it.
detour @0x00B4 to Talked If var[2CE] & #-32768 else @0x00C4

message Concern
{
Oh dear...{wait 14}
He's been taking your
money too, hasn't he?
}
message Offer
{
I could have a little talk
with him... He'll go easy
on you after that, I'm sure.
    Yes, please!
    No, I'll beat him myself!
}
message Accepted
{
Leave it to me.{wait 14}
He's never once won an
argument with me, you know.
}
message Declined
{
My, how brave...{wait 14}
Well, do try not to lose
the whole house.
}

; The player's choice (rock, scissors and paper: lines 1 to 3) and the man's hand (0 rock, 1 scissors, 2 paper).
define Choice var[014]
define HisHand var[408]
; The helper's state, in scratch variables no map's scripts use and every entry clears, so that saves stay
; the game's: the rounds the visit has lost, and whether she has had her word.
define Losses var[7FC]
define Helped var[7FE]

; After Fei's choice. Once she has had her word, the man forgets Fei's runs of a hand and plays the hand
; Fei's beats: rock and scissors beat his scissors (1) and paper (2), the choice's own numbers, and paper
; beats his rock (0).
Hands:
    If Helped == #1 else Played
    SetVarFalse var[40C]
    SetVarFalse var[40E]
    SetVarFalse var[410]
    SetVar HisHand Choice
    If Choice == #3 else Played
    SetVarFalse HisHand
Played:
    If Choice == #1 else @0x0329
    Jump @0x0252

; After a lost round: one more for the visit.
Lost:
    IncVar Losses
    Jump @0x059B

; Talked to after the visit's third loss and before she has had her word, she offers it in place of her
; lines. The offer's choice is its fourth and fifth lines, yes first.
Talked:
    If Helped == #0 else HerLines
    If Losses > #2 else HerLines
    Message Concern 0
    WaitMessageClosed
    Message Offer 0
    MessageChoice 0x34
    WaitMessageClosed
    If Choice == #3 else NoThanks
    Message Accepted 0
    WaitMessageClosed
    SetVarTrue Helped
    Jump @0x00C9
NoThanks:
    Message Declined 0
    WaitMessageClosed
    Jump @0x00C9
HerLines:
    If var[2CE] & #-32768 else @0x00C4
    Jump @0x00BC
